Usage of Electric Vehicle in the United States

This interactive visualization explores the distribution of Electric Vehicle Supply Equipment (EVSE) across the United States. The choropleth map shows normalized EVSE per 100,000 people for fair state-to-state comparison, while the bar chart displays total EVSE by region. Use the Region filter to focus on specific areas and the Reexpress dropdown to toggle between normalized rates, total counts, population, or charger types. Hover over any state or bar to reveal detailed information about EV infrastructure patterns and regional disparities.
